## Slide 5: The machine learning

**On the slide:** A model that says when it does not know: "Insufficient experimental evidence for a reliable estimate."

**Speaker notes:** One Gaussian-process model per material setup (9 modelled). It separates lack of data from outcome variability and gives a probability only where tests support it. Pressure barely varies in the data, so anything else rests on an assumption the app states. Screenshot: the answer card for the Exploration Atmosphere (`docs/img/cabins-answer.png`).

## Slide 6: What to learn next

**On the slide:** What to test next: 34.0 % O2, 56.5 kPa, 1.71 cm/s would cut the model's uncertainty about this cabin by 19.3%. Hypothetical model updates, not experimental results.

**Speaker notes:** Active learning ranks candidates by expected uncertainty cut for the chosen cabin. Same model, other strategies: random 0.0%, uncertainty sampling 0.0%, nearest gap 15.8%. The top-ranked candidate sits at the target conditions themselves; this follows from the model's distance-based (stationary) correlation assumption, because no integrated observation constrains that region. Honest validation: the model beats a majority-class guess in 6 of 9 material setups; the ranking beat random in 11 of 18 retrospective scenarios (worse in 2). A computational research candidate requiring qualified scientific review, not a test order.
