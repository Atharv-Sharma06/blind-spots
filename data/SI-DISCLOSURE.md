- `data/raw/`: NASA source records (NASA Physical Sciences Informatics, CC0) and NASA reports. Not SI-generated.
- `data/curation/`: tables that transcribe NASA source cells and report tables. Drafted with SI assistance
  (Claude, Anthropic) and checked by code: every curated token is matched exactly against its cited NASA source
  cell (`scripts/check_curation_provenance.py`).
- `data/normalized/` and `models/`: generated by the project's code (itself SI-assisted) from the files above.
  No values were typed in by an SI tool; every number is computed from NASA data.
